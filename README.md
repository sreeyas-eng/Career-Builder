# 🎓 AI Career Buddy

**Discover Your Skills. Build Your Future.**

AI Career Buddy is an AI-powered career assistant designed for college students and fresh graduates. It helps users prepare for internships and job opportunities through personalized career guidance, resume building, and interview preparation.

Powered by **Google Gemini AI**, it brings essential career development tools together in one platform.

## 🚀 Features

- **AI Resume Builder:** Creates ATS-friendly resumes based on academic details, skills, and projects.
- **Interview Questions:** Generates role-specific technical and HR interview questions.
- **Answer Improver:** Enhances interview answers using the STAR method with feedback and scores.
- **Career Guidance:** Suggests suitable career paths based on interests, skills, and education.
- **Skill Learning Roadmap:** Creates personalized Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced learning plans.
- **Cover Letter Generator:** Generates professional, job-specific cover letters.
